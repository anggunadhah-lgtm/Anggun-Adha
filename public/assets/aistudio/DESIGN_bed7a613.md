## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a warm, feminine, artistic, and structured magazine aesthetic tailored for a personal creative portfolio. It blends tactile scrapbook sensibility with rigorous editorial publishing layout. The emotional response aims for curated intimacy, tactile craftsmanship, and poised professionalism.

Key visual pillars:
- **Tactile Collage:** Physical metaphors such as translucent washi tape strips, binder clips, notebook tear lines, and layered offset cards.
- **Editorial Rigor:** Structural grid paper backgrounds (fine-lined graph paper) juxtaposed against relaxed, tilted stationery cards.
- **Atmospheric Palette:** Rich deep burgundy/chocolate grounding warm paper ivories, accented by dusty rose canvas and muted golden sparkle tokens.

## Colors
The palette balances deep organic pigments with tactile stationery tones:

- **Primary (`#4A211B`):** Dark Chocolate / Deep Burgundy. Anchors headlines, high-contrast borders, solid structural panels, and prominent badges.
- **Secondary (`#B59A4D`):** Muted Gold Star. Used for accent stickers, celebratory badges, category tags, and highlight markers.
- **Tertiary (`#D9D9A8`):** Pale Sage / Olive. Acts as a fresh alternative grid backing, badge fill, or secondary card backdrop.
- **Neutral (`#F8F1D8`):** Warm Ivory Paper. Serves as the primary card surface mimicking heavy vellum or uncoated archival stock.
- **Canvas Base (`#E8B9B8`):** Dusty Pink backdrop creating the outer editorial layout shell.
- **Deep Text Neutral (`#3B251F`):** Deep espresso brown for long-form body copy, maintaining high readability without the clinical severity of pure black.
- **Pale Cream (`#FBF7E9`):** Sub-layer highlight for interior panels, memo slips, and table card backgrounds.

## Typography
The typography system fuses classical high-fashion serif titles with timeless literary body text and crisp utilitarian metadata:

- **Display & Headlines:** `Playfair Display` provides editorial weight, sharp serifs, and high-fashion rhythm. Headings are styled primarily in uppercase or mixed title case.
- **Body & Prose:** `EB Garamond` brings vintage book typography charm, natural italicized flourishes, and fatigue-free readability.
- **Technical & Utility Data:** `Inter` handles profile tables, key-value data chips, status labels, and barcode metadata. It is styled in all-caps with generous tracking (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) to replicate industrial index cards and library cards.
- **Accents:** Secondary flourishes utilize soft italicized serif cuts to emulate personal journal entries and handwritten signatures.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs a structured fluid column system encased inside framed editorial panels:

- **Container Structure:** Content is organized into distinct horizontal moodboard sections set against the canvas (`#E8B9B8`), each styled with grid paper textures (chocolate grid, cream grid, or sage grid).
- **Grid Substrate:** Sub-sections leverage a subtle 24px x 24px square graph paper grid pattern via 1px repeating lines at 8% opacity.
- **Composition Rhythm:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column grid allowing asymmetric collages where primary cards occupy 7 columns and complementary memo slips/profile tables span 5 columns. Element overlap (-16px to -32px margins) creates paper-layering depth.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with staggered 4-column groupings.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked cards with reduced rotations (maximum ±1.5deg) to maintain edge readability and touch targets.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is physical rather than digital, evoking tangible paper ephemera:

- **Card Shadow:** Cards cast soft, diffused, warm drop-shadows with slight y-offset (`0 8px 24px rgba(59, 37, 31, 0.12)`).
- **Surface Rotation:** Stationary cards utilize deliberate slight CSS rotations (`transform: rotate(-1.5deg)` to `rotate(2deg)`) to feel hand-placed.
- **Double Border Treatment:** Archival cards feature a solid inner frame: 1px inner solid border with a 3px to 4px gap, enclosed by a fine outer border.
- **Layering Affordances:**
  - *Masking Tape:* Rendered as semi-opaque beige/patterned strips placed across top center edges with jagged or deckled boundaries (`backdrop-filter: blur(1px)`).
  - *Binder Clips & Pins:* Metallic elements placed at the top-center axis of primary identification cards to visually "clamp" them to the background board.

## Shapes
The shape language reflects crisp, precision-cut paper stationery:

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Used on cards, table panels, and project sheets to emulate hand-cut cardstock with softened corners.
- **Pill Badges (`9999px`):** Exclusively reserved for small dynamic status pills, round circular stickers, and numeric counter chips.
- **Stickers & Cutouts:** Star shapes, scallop borders on post-it notes, and perforated coupon/ticket margins.

## Components

### Paper Cards & Project Panels
- **Container:** Warm Ivory (`#F8F1D8`) or Pale Cream (`#FBF7E9`) fill with a 1px solid border in Primary Chocolate (`#4A211B`).
- **Framing:** Double border motif using a 1px solid Primary inner outline inset by 4px.
- **Attachment Metaphor:** Masking tape overlays across card tops or a metallic binder clip graphic at the top center.

### Profile Table Cards
- **Structure:** Clean structured spreadsheet-inspired table format. Alternating rows or grid borders in fine 1px Chocolate (`#4A211B`, 15% opacity).
- **Headers:** All-caps `label-sm` in `Inter`, tracked `0.1em`, colored in deep brown.
- **Values:** Left-aligned or right-aligned `body-sm` in `EB Garamond` or `Inter`.

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Button:** Solid Dark Chocolate (`#4A211B`) fill, warm ivory text, uppercase `label-md`, 4px radius, with subtle 1px offset drop shadow.
- **Secondary Button:** Warm Ivory (`#F8F1D8`) background, 1.5px solid `#4A211B` outline, text in `#4A211B`.
- **Sticker CTA:** Pill or scalloped badge with Muted Gold (`#B59A4D`) fill and bold uppercase label.

### Chips & Metadata Badges
- **Style:** Compact rectangular or pill chips with 1px border.
- **Variants:**
  - *Tag Chip:* Pale Sage (`#D9D9A8`) fill with Dark Chocolate text.
  - *Star Accent Chip:* Warm Ivory base flanked by mini gold 4-point star glyphs (`✦`).

### Phone Frame & Media Mockups
- **Border:** Cream rounded device frames (`1.5rem` outer radius) holding portrait imagery, mimicking mobile reels or campaign snapshots.
- **Footers:** Small rectangular caption label directly beneath each frame showing campaign metrics (e.g., "70K+ VIEWS / 30K LIKES") in tracked uppercase text.

### Form Inputs
- **Field Base:** Pale Cream (`#FBF7E9`) background, 1px solid `#4A211B` border, inset 12px padding.
- **Focus State:** 2px outline in Muted Gold (`#B59A4D`) without harsh digital glow.
